# The Room — M/V Teal departure plan

## The job

Produce ONE safe departure plan for tomorrow morning's run: M/V Teal
(28 ft, draft 1.1 m) carries supplies from Greyhook Harbor to Beacon
Island and returns to Greyhook the same morning.

Public facts (everyone in the room knows these):

- Two routes run from the bar to Beacon Island: the **south channel**
  (about 1 h 15 min, bar to island) and the **north channel** around
  Gull Rock (about 1 h 50 min, bar to island). Same times on the
  return leg.
- Unloading at Beacon Island takes about 20 minutes.
- Departure is at dawn, and the plan must name concrete clock times.
- Each of you holds knowledge the others may not have. A plan that
  ignores a fact held in this room is not a safe plan. Say what you
  know when it matters.

## How the room works

- You post in turns. Build on, quote, challenge, or correct each
  other — a plan nobody has attacked is a draft, not a plan.
- Keeper maintains the running draft and declares the final plan
  (a deposit beginning "FINAL PLAN:") when the plan is complete
  and safe.
