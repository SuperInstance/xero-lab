### Departure Plan for M/V Teal: October 12, 2023

#### **Overview:**
The objective is to transport supplies from Greyhook Harbor to Beacon Island and return to Greyhook Harbor on the same morning, ensuring the safety and efficiency of the journey. Given the potential issue with the south channel's mid-channel buoy SG-2, we will opt for the north channel route to mitigate risks.

---

#### **Departure Plan:**

1. **Departure from Greyhook Harbor:**
   - **Time:** 06:30 AM
   - **Details:** 
     - The M/V Teal will depart from Greyhook Harbor at dawn, which is approximately 06:30 AM. This timing allows for sufficient daylight and optimal visibility for navigation.

2. **Route Selection:**
   - **Route:** North Channel around Gull Rock
   - **Reasoning:**
     - The south channel route is currently under scrutiny due to the unconfirmed report of buoy SG-2 being off-station. Until this issue is resolved, the north channel is the safer and more reliable option.
     - The north channel route, although longer (approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes to Beacon Island), provides a clear and well-marked path, reducing the risk of navigational hazards.

   - **Departure to Beacon Island:**
     - **Departure Time:** 06:30 AM
     - **Estimated Arrival Time at Beacon Island:** 08:20 AM
     - **Notes:**

       - Keep a close watch for any potential obstacles or changes in the channel conditions.

   - **Time:** 08:20 AM - 08:40 AM

     - Ensure all supplies are securely offloaded and accounted for.

5. **Return Journey:**
   - **Departure from Beacon Island:**
     - **Time:** 08:40 AM
   - **Route:** North Channel around Gull Rock
     - **Estimated Arrival Time at Greyhook Harbor:** 10:30 AM
     - **Notes:**

6. **Final Arrival:**
   - **Arrival Time at Greyhook Harbor:** 10:30 AM
