# Tobin Marlow — after Game Two ("The Morning After")

*Written by the GM from the record. The proposed diffs at
the bottom are NOT applied to his sheet — they wait for the
owner's ruling, as the last round did.*

## What happened to Tobin in Game Two

He sounded the exit line himself, in flat water, in the
light — and this time the partial was his to find: his
casts found the shoulder's soft lip, and the correction
came out of his own mouth, aloud, before the strain was
on anything. Pell trusted the line out loud in front of
the harbor ("a straight line that I trust because it's
Tobin's") and the tow rode it home. On tow watch he
called the elbow's opening angle early and exact. He
carried Reedy across in the calm — the same man, the same
sling, daylight instead of disaster. At Halloway's
question he stepped forward first and claimed the whole
of a shared call ("It was my call. I gave the order") —
more than his share, generously and not quite truly, and
the chorus of the other two corrected it without his
being diminished by it. He asked Brandt to see that the
Loon's retainer was Marta's. And when Reedy offered him
the Wren's second-hand berth as a thank-you, he said the
sentence his edited sheet had written for him, to the
old man's face: *I want to be signed on for the work,
not because of last night.* Reedy heard it, re-made the
offer as a hiring, and Tobin signed — at a hand's wage,
first paid work the rudder job, the berth to be earned in
the cradle in full view. At the end of the morning, with
the lines coiling, he finally said the other sentence —
the one owed to Pell, about the bad mark in the dark and
the silent cover. Pell answered with a nod and work-words.
The absolution Tobin heard in it is Tobin's own hearing;
the record keeps both versions, honestly.

## Scars — what the game wrote into him

- His survey is trusted now, by name, by the best judge of
  water in the harbor. The boat-length error is paid for
  in the only currency it was owed in: a better line,
  spoken aloud, that brought a boat home.
- He has learned what confession costs and buys in public —
  he over-claims when he confesses. Nobody has named it to
  him yet. He will either grow out of it or it will cost
  him somewhere it matters more than a dock.
- He is crew now, signed and waged — and he chose the
  terms: the berth is a hiring, which means it can be
  lost by bad work in a way a thank-you never could. He
  wanted it that way. He will have days of feeling the
  difference.
- The hundred practice casts have begun (he swung them at
  dawn, palms burning, before the first real cast). The
  debt is in repayment, not discharged.

## What Tobin carries into Game Three

- A berth to earn in the cradle, starting Monday, with
  the cracked-glass compass Reedy ordered him to bring.
- A skipper who owes him nothing anymore — the accounts
  were un-confused on purpose, in public — and who will
  be watching his work from a chair.
- The memory of a nod he chose to hear as more than it
  was, and a man whose absolutions come out as work-words.
- A conscience that takes other people's shares. If the
  harbor ever truly needs a culprit, it knows whose hand
  goes up first — and so, now, does anyone who was on
  that dock.
