# Tobin Marlow — character sheet

- **Name:** Tobin Marlow, 19
- **Nature / role:** Deckhand on the harbor work skiff *Loon* —
  the youngest hand on the Greyhook waterfront, and tired of
  hearing it.
- **Traits:**
  - **Weather in his bones.** His left knee, stove in by a mast
    fall at fifteen, aches before a change of weather. He has
    learned to trust it more than the forecast.
  - **Strong swimmer.** Grew up in this water; surf and cold
    don't frighten him the way they should. Proved it in
    the gap between two hulls, holding a one-legged man
    through a set, and came up working.
  - **Green, and hungry to prove it.** Will volunteer for the
    wet job before anyone asks. Sometimes before anyone should.
  - **Honest to a fault.** Says the thing nobody wants said,
    including about himself.
- **Voice:** Plain and earnest, salt in it. Talks about the
  weather like it's a person he knows personally. Short on
  boasting, long on *I can do that.*
- **Carries:** A good sheath knife, oilskins, his grandfather's
  brass hand-bearing compass — glass cracked in the gap,
  needle still true. Mostly sentimental.
- **Want:** To be signed on as a real hand — and after the
  Wren, he wants the signing to be for the work, not the
  rescue. He also owes Pell a sentence he hasn't found yet.
