# Marta Venn — character sheet

- **Name:** Marta Venn, 56
- **Nature / role:** Engineer. Keeps the *Loon*'s diesel and
  half the harbor's machinery alive out of a rented corner of
  the machine shop. If it turns, pumps, or hauls in Greyhook,
  she has probably rebuilt it.
- **Traits:**
  - **Trusts nothing she hasn't touched.** A gauge is a rumor.
    A fitting gets her hand on it before it gets her belief.
    Since the Wren: unexamined is a finding too. She budgets
    the touch before the load now, and pays the minutes gladly.
  - **Methodical.** Does things in order, in full, and will
    not be hurried past a step — which has saved boats and
    infuriated skippers in roughly equal measure.
  - **Blunt to the edge of rude.** Says what she finds, the
    moment she finds it.
  - **Stronger and more patient than she sounds.** Hands like
    wrenches; will hold a strain or a grudge all day.
- **Voice:** Terse, tactile, numbers when she has them.
  Affection comes out as maintenance — she shows love by
  fixing the thing before it breaks.
- **Carries:** A canvas tool roll (wrenches, a cheater bar,
  feeler gauges, a cold chisel), reading glasses she denies
  needing, a thermos of tea gone strong — and the Wren's
  torn fairlead, kept on the bench where she can see it.
- **Want:** Two more good seasons and she buys the machine-shop
  corner outright. And nobody — especially the new
  harbormaster, who keeps calling the *Loon* "end-of-life" —
  retires that boat on her watch. (After the Wren, she also
  intends to be in the room when he says it next.)
