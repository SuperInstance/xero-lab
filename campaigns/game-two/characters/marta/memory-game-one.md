# Marta Venn — after Game One

*Written by the GM from the record (her log, the scene,
the dice). This is the persistence file: who she is now,
before anyone edits anything.*

## What happened to her

Her hands found everything true aboard the *Wren* — the
stiff winch, the sound windlass, the unscarred hull — and
missed the one thing that mattered, because June called
her below and the light was going and the moment passed
(roll 1, +1 by her own trait, still a failure). The sea
taught it an hour later by tearing the fairlead out half
a second after her eyes caught it going (roll 6 — flat on
the deck, untouched, holding the evidence). From there
she was the game's spine: splinted Reedy in a minute,
gave June a minute and a job-sized kindness and got her
back, coached the girl's first windlass run clean
(roll 6), lent the haul the strength that turned it
(roll 4, +1 her trait → 5: the one band a trait changed
all night), and took the *Wren*'s helm for the tow home
that ended on the mud — correctly.

## Scars — what persists

- **The fairlead.** She kept it. It sits on her machine-
  shop bench where she can see it: wasted bolts, soft as
  cheese, a part that announced itself for years to anyone
  who touched it. *Unexamined is a finding too* is about
  to become something Greyhook hears her say.
- **A slower first heave, forever.** She will budget the
  touch *before* the load now, and pay the minutes
  gladly, and let skippers fume.
- **A revised ledger of people.** June Park: trusted.
  Pell Sutter: his hands knew the re-lead cold, and she
  has quietly stopped believing his dock act.

## What she carries into the next game

- The torn fairlead, on the bench.
- A debt owed *to* her by events: the harbormaster came
  home twenty minutes after he was any use, and the
  *Loon* — "end-of-life," his word — brought everybody
  back. She intends to collect on that, in berth fees
  and in public.
- Opinions about the *Wren*'s rudder, already formed,
  which Reedy will be hearing at length.
