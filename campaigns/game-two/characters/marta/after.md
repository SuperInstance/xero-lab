# Marta Venn — after Game Two ("The Morning After")

*Written by the GM from the record. The proposed diffs at
the bottom are NOT applied to her sheet — they wait for
the owner's ruling, as the last round did.*

## What happened to Marta in Game Two

She ran the morning the way her edited sheet says she
runs things now: hands before load, in her order, every
fitting the tow would trust — rudder stock, pintles,
sweep, cleats — before any strain was on anything. Her
thumb found what her eyes had just reported absent: the
Wren's port quarter cleat, the fitting the stern
check-line wanted, cracked most of the way through under
its paint. She condemned it in the open at 06:50 and the
check-line went to the kingpost. In the channel, at the
Gull Rock elbow, the set threw the tow's whole snatch
onto that kingpost — the exact load the cleat was
cracked for. It held. She did the arithmetic herself, at
the helm, and said nothing, which is its own entry in the
ledger. She conned a rudderless boat home, stood forward
at Halloway's question beside the two boys and shut his
arithmetic down with the plain record, counselled Tobin
mid-offer (in parallel with Pell, neither knowing), and
then — in the room, pen in reach — heard Brandt call the
Loon "end-of-life" for what may be the last time, and
answered it standing before she signed the standby
retainer under the sentence: real monthly money,
backdated, toward her corner. Before noon she had a
wrench on the condemned cleat, the Wren's new second hand
sent for the toolbox, and the doctrine already becoming
someone else's training.

## Scars — what the game wrote into her

- The doctrine is proven now — not as penance but as
  measurement: her touch caught in the morning what her
  eyes missed at dawn, and the channel provided the
  control experiment an hour later. It will be harder for
  her to watch anyone skip the touch now, including
  people she likes.
- She is becoming a teacher despite herself: the cleat
  came off with Tobin on the toolbox, and her rule —
  *unexamined is a finding too* — is on its way to being
  harbor doctrine with her name filed off it.
- The corner is measurably closer: retainer money is not
  a dream figure anymore, it's a monthly line in the
  harbor book. Her want acquired arithmetic.
- She watched a berth refused as a thank-you and re-made
  as a hiring, and approved of both men in it. Her ledger
  of people gained a column she didn't plan: what they do
  when gratitude is offered instead of wages.

## What Marta carries into Game Three

- The retainer: monthly harbor money toward the machine-
  shop corner, and the Loon's standing in the book to
  match her standing on the water.
- A bench with two pieces of torn metal once the cleat
  joins the fairlead: the one she missed, and the one she
  caught. The pair is the whole doctrine in objects.
- The Wren's repair season: cradle, rudder, chafed
  lashings renewed — and a green second hand to train in
  her order of work, on a boat whose skipper is in a
  chair.
- Pell Sutter's hands, still unexplained: the first crate
  heaved before the decision finished, the competence
  that doesn't match the act. She has said nothing. She
  is still watching, and the watching is closer than it
  was.
