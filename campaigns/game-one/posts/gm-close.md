people aboard her and nobody saying much of anything, the
way crews don't after the water has had its say and lost.

lay. Inside Gull Rock she sits in the mud with her stern
light lit, her skipper asleep under blankets with a
splinted ankle, her deckhand watching the bilge lamp like
a hawk, dry as a bone. Tomorrow they'll float her off at
the tide and tow her home like a lady, and Marta Venn
already has opinions about her rudder.
