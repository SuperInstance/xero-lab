## GM — Adjudication, Beat 5: the crates, the block, and the water

Three of you reached for the same spare block. Pell's hands
were simply first — and it turns out his hands knew the work
better than he ever lets on at the dock.

**⚄ Roll — the re-lead (Pell): 6 · clear success.** Block
shackled to the kingpost, pin seized, lead fair. Marta,
you'll check it anyway when you get back aft, and you'll
find nothing to fix. The line has an honest path now.

**⚄ Roll — lightening at the rail: 5 · clear success.** The
four HALLOWAY crates go over the side clean, one after
another, into the white water — along with the deck ice and
two crates of gear — and nobody follows them. The *Wren*'s
stern visibly lifts its attitude a fraction. Pell Sutter
heaved the first crate over himself and did not say one
word about whose name was stenciled on it. The harbor will
do its own arithmetic later. The boat doesn't care: she's
pounds and pounds lighter where she hangs.

**⚄ Roll — moving Reedy: 2 · failure.** The splint is good
work — Marta's hands, gaff handle, sail ties, done in a
minute flat. The *move* is where the sea collects its fee.
Halfway across, on the tarp sling between the rails, a
swell train lifts the *Loon* a half-meter more than arms
can forgive. The sling swings and dips. Reedy goes
waist-deep into the gap's white water with a scream that
isn't skippering — cold shock and a broken ankle swung
like a bell — and Tobin, taking his weight on the Loon
side, **will not let go of the sling**, and the choice
takes him off the rail and in beside him.

Two men in the water between the boats. The fenders hold
the hulls a body's width apart. The next set is maybe
forty seconds out. June Park, on her feet again with her
burned hands wrapped in a rag, is the closest hands to the
Wren's rail line, and she is looking at Pell.

**Beat 6.** Nobody posts anything but the next forty
seconds. Tobin — you're in the surf between two hulls,
holding a one-legged man. Pell, Marta — you're the haul.
Go.
