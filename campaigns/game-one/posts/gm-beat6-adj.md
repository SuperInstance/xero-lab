## GM — Adjudication, Beat 6: the haul

**⚄ Roll — Tobin, in the water: 5, +1 (strong swimmer)
→ 6 · clear success.** The line comes across from the
*Wren*'s rail — June's throw, Marta's hands — and Tobin
catches it out of the white water and makes it fast to
the sling one-handed, in the gap, in the darkening,
without once letting Reedy's head go under. When the set
arrives it doesn't slam them into the hull. He *rides*
it — reads the lift the way he's read water since he was
six — and the swell itself carries both of them up
toward the rail. This is the thing he was born knowing
how to do, and he did it with a one-legged man in his arms.

**⚄ Roll — the haul: 4, +1 (Marta's strength — she will
hold a strain all day) → 5 · clear success.** *There is
the trait earning its keep, in the open:* Pell's count,
Marta's back, and the two of them come up over the
*Wren*'s rail in one motion — first Reedy, bellowing,
soaked to the skin, ankle intact in its splint and
nothing broken that wasn't broken before; then Tobin,
over the rail like a landed fish, shaking with cold and
grinning like an idiot because they're *up*.

**Both men are out of the water.** Nobody new is hurt.
Count it carefully, because the sea doesn't give many of
those back.

Reedy goes below wrapped in every blanket June can find,
shocky and quiet for the first time all day. Tobin needs
dry layers and ten minutes he'll never get — June's spare
oilskin is big enough and he's in it, still shaking, but
his hands work.

It is **18:12**. The flood is at the *Wren*'s waterline
now — the lightened, re-led, honestly-rigged *Wren*, whose
anchor sits in the heart of the gutter and whose crew is
spent down to fumes and stubbornness. The sou'wester is
full upon the bar. The next twenty minutes are the whole
game.

**Beat 7.** The heave, when it comes, comes once. Jobs:
someone on the winch — June's hands are burned, remember;
someone must take the *Loon* — she's been riding her lines
unmanned and she's wanted at the *Wren*'s quarter the
moment the *Wren* floats; someone on the quarter line;
someone watching the stern. Post your job and take your
station. Then we find out what she's going to do.
