I crouch by the windlass, feeling the deck shift under me as the swells grow. The wind's picking up, carrying the scent of rain. June stands ready, her hands on the lever, eyes fixed on me. "Keep it steady," I tell her, my voice firm but not unkind. "Feel for the resistance, and don't fight it. Let the machine do the work."

I glance at the sky, noting the dark clouds rolling in. Time's running short. "When I give the word, you start cranking. Slow and even." I check the line one last time, making sure it's clear and ready to run. The windlass is old, but it's held so far. I trust it about as much as I trust anything I haven't rebuilt myself.

Below, Reedy's still barking orders, but I tune him out. He's in no shape to be calling the shots. I nod to June, meeting her eyes. "Now." As she starts cranking, I keep my hands close, ready to jump in if anything goes wrong. The windlass groans, but it holds. I just hope the anchor bites before the storm hits.
