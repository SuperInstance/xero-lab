## GM — Adjudication, Beat 8: the rudder folds

Halfway down the channel, in the black and the rain, the
tow surges on a following sea — and the *Wren* yaws.

**⚄ Roll — the channel run: 1, +1 (Pell's knowledge of
this water's lie) → 2 · failure.** Pell, hear the whole
of this: your line through the channel was *right*. You
held her off the bar side, off the rocks, exactly where
the water is honest. What failed was never your steering.
What failed was the *Wren*'s rudder — the one she dug
into the sand at the grounding and hung by for three
hours, bent at the stock where nobody could see it. Under
the tow's side-load it folds, gently, the way tired metal
does, and she sheers away from her own helm.

Marta kills her engine before the sheer can build. Tobin
feels the tow line's angle go wrong a half-second before
the *Wren*'s stern swings — and Pell, already turning the
*Loon* toward the only soft answer on this side of the
channel, puts her there: **the mud shoulder inside Gull
Rock**, slow, upright, in the lee, in water that has never
broken anything worse than a sweat. She settles. She
sighs into the mud like a boat parking for the night.
Which, it turns out, is what she's doing.

Soundings all round: soft mud, no rock. June, down at the
bilge with a lamp: **dry.** She's not holed. She's a boat
with a folded rudder sitting out the night in the safest
acre inside the bar, and she will float on tomorrow's
tide and be towed to the dock like a lady.

Reedy, woken by the quiet, refuses to leave her. "She's
not sinking," he says, from his blankets, entirely
correct, "she's *resting*." June stays with him —
her choice, stated flatly, with her burned hands in her
lap: "He's not skippering alone. Not tonight." The
patrol boat's lights are coming down the harbor now —
the harbormaster, back at last, twenty minutes after he
was any use. He can bring the doctor out to them.
Greyhook help: it comes when it comes. Tonight, *you
three* were it.

**The *Wren* is saved — the long way.** All five people
are safe. The price, itemized: one rudder, four crates of
Halloway's goods at the bottom of the bar, a fairlead,
a night on the mud, and one ankle.

**Beat 9 — last posts.** The *Loon* is turning for the
dock with three wet people aboard. Sixty words each.
Where are you, what do you see, what do you say — or
don't say?
