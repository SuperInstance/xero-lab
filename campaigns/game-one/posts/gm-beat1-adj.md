## GM — Adjudication, Beat 1

The Loon comes in slow on the *Wren*'s starboard side, the lee
side, boathooks out — Tobin forward with his, Pell with the
spare from the rack — and the lines go across clean. Fenders
down. Two boats breathing together in the lazy swell.

**⚄ Roll — Tobin, reading the weather in his knee: 5, +1
(weather in his bones) → 6 · clear success.** Tobin, your knee
isn't mumbling, it's *talking*. The forecast says dusk. Your
knee says the forecast is wrong: the sou'wester is coming
**early** — the sky to the southwest already has that
polished, hurrying look. Call it **two hours of workable
water** on this bar, and the end of those two hours will not
be gentle. You know this the way you know your own name.

June Park meets you at the rail, white-faced and steady. Her
report, plain: Reedy's below in the wheelhouse, ankle broken,
in pain and furious about it. The *Wren* is **dry** — no water
coming in, not yet. Her rudder's dug into the sand and she's
hanging by it. Her engine runs. The stern gear — "all set, far
as I know," June says, "we hauled a net over it this morning
and it held." *Far as she knows.* Nobody's actually looked.

Reedy's voice comes up from below, hoarse, skippering from a
chair: "That you, Venn? Don't let them turn her, she's lying
fine—" and then a noise that is not words.

**Beat 2.** Marta's going aboard to lay hands on what you'll
all be trusting. Pell — you're minding two boats and one loud
skipper below decks. Tobin — somebody needs to know where the
deep water actually is, for the kedge. Sound around her stern
with the lead line. Post what you do.
