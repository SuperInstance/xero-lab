# Pell Sutter — character sheet

- **Name:** Pell Sutter, 20
- **Nature / role:** The harbor kid. Grew up under these docks,
  runs errands, crews when there's pay, day-works wherever a
  boat is short a body. There is no dock, boat, or piece of
  waterfront business in Greyhook he doesn't know.
- **Traits:**
  - **Knows every dock and most secrets.** Who's feuding, who
    owes, which winch screams, which channel lies at low
    water — he has it all filed away.
  - **Fast talker.** Can borrow almost anything exactly once.
    Charm offensive is his first tool and his best one.
  - **Slippery when cornered.** Changes the subject, changes
    the plan, changes the room if he can. Debt, blame, and
    direct questions all get the same treatment.
  - **A better boat-hand than he lets on.** Competence gets
    you volunteered, so he rations it — but his hands know
    the work when nobody's asking.
- **Voice:** Quick, funny, name-dropping. The jokes get faster
  when he's scared. Underneath, he keeps accounts nobody sees.
- **Carries:** A borrowed phone (cracked screen), a multitool,
  a headlamp, exactly eleven dollars.
- **Want:** The **$400 he owes Halloway** the chandlery owner
  — for a seized-solid outboard he bought last spring to flip,
  a sure thing that wasn't — **gone**, before Halloway calls
  it in at month's end. Nobody on the waterfront knows but
  Halloway. Pell intends to keep it that way.
