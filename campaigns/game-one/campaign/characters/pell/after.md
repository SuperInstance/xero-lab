# Pell Sutter — after Game One

*Written by the GM from the record (his log, the scene,
the dice). This is the persistence file: who he is now,
before anyone edits anything. The GM notes that the most
important entry in it is one Pell has told nobody.*

## What happened to him

Waved aboard as a third pair of hands, he became the
crew's connective tissue. He conned the *Loon* onto the
heart of the gutter by feel, silently covering Tobin's
off mark (roll 5, +1 his knowledge of the water → 6).
He re-led the kedge line through the kingpost in the
worst minutes of the night and did it *perfectly*
(roll 6) — the engineer's job, done by the harbor kid's
hands, while the engineer was busy saving the skipper.
He called the count that hauled two men out of the gap.
He took the *Loon* for the climax, and when the *Wren*'s
rudder folded in the channel he put her on the mud
inside Gull Rock like parking a car (a failure roll —
his line through the channel was never the problem).

And at the lightening, with the whole crew shouting for
the heavy deck cargo to go, Pell Sutter took hold of the
first HALLOWAY crate himself — his creditor's goods, his
secret, four hundred dollars and then some — and heaved
it into the sea. He said nothing about whose name was on
it. Before, during, or after. He still hasn't.

## Scars — what persists

- **The crates are on the bottom of the bar**, and
  Halloway will learn what the *Wren* was carrying the
  moment Reedy files his report. Whether Halloway ever
  learns *whose hands* is now the live question of
  Pell's life. The debt didn't shrink tonight. It changed
  species: from money owed to a thing concealed.
- **The blown cover.** His whole dock act rests on
  rationing his competence. Three people watched his
  hands know the work all night. Marta Venn, at minimum,
  has stopped believing the act — and Marta tells the
  truth to people's faces.
- **A skipper's IOU.** Reedy knows who called the count
  that got him out of the water. In Greyhook that's
  currency — if Pell ever dares spend it anywhere
  Halloway can hear about it.

## What he carries into the next game

- Eleven dollars. Still. The multitool, the headlamp,
  the borrowed phone.
- A secret heavier than the debt was.
- A crew that trusts him more than he meant to allow.
