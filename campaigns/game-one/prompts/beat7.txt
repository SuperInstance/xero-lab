lightened Wren's waterline; the sou'wester is fully on the
bar. Both men are out of the water — Reedy below in
blankets, Tobin soaked and shaking in June's spare oilskin
but his hands work. The kedge is set in the gutter's heart.
The hauling line is re-led fairly through the kingpost.
When the heave comes, it comes once.
