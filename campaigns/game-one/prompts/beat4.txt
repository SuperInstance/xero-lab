Beat 4. The Loon is back alongside the Wren. A train of
bigger swells is coming down on the bar and the wind is
rising on their backs — the sou'wester arriving ahead of
its forecast. The crew takes its first real heave on the
kedge line: the windlass hauls, the Wren's stern should walk
around toward deep water, and everyone learns what the gear
will stand.

Post where you are for the heave and what your hands are
doing:
- If you're Marta: you're calling the heave and standing
  where you can see the line, the winch, and June.
- If you're Tobin: you're tending the line's lead aft, or
  where your post puts you — say it plainly.
- If you're Pell: you're with the Loon, ready to take the
  Wren's quarter line and keep the boats working together
  when she moves — or say where else you've put yourself.

~120 words, in your voice. End on your action; the GM
resolves the heave.
