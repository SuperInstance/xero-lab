Using ONLY the character sheet you were given as the whole of your
world, write your life so far — the life that produced this person,
up to this morning. First person, in your own voice, about 200
words. Ground it in the sheet: your traits, what you carry, and
what you want should each have a history — show how you got them,
not just that you have them. Greyhook Harbor is your home. Don't
invent a different character. Become this one. Write only the
life — no title, no preamble.
