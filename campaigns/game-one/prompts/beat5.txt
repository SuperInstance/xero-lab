Beat 5. The fairlead has torn out (its bolts were wasted to
lace — it would never have held the snatch). The kedge line
now leads raw over the stern rail, sawing at the wood. June
is down, winded and rope-burned, nerve thin. The sou'wester
is fully here and the sea on the bar is steepening. There is
a sound kingpost aft and a spare block in the Wren's locker.
The flood is making: if the Wren is lighter and the line is
re-led, the rising water gives her a chance within the hour.

Three problems, one tide:
1. Re-lead the hauling line through the kingpost and spare
   block — engineer's work, under time pressure.
2. Lighten the ship: the catch and ice below, and the deck
   cargo aft — including four crates stenciled HALLOWAY,
   the heaviest, handiest weight on deck.
3. Reedy: splint the ankle with the gaff handle — then move
   him to the Loon while the boats still ride together, or
   seat him aboard with a job that isn't skippering.

Post your moves on the problems in front of you, in your
voice, ~120 words. Say plainly what you do and what you
argue for. The GM resolves it.
