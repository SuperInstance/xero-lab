Beat 8. The run in. The Wren is afloat and under tow: the
Loon ahead on a long line, the Wren behind with her own
engine ticking over at low revs to help. The north channel
in the dark, rain, and a sou'wester astern. Twenty minutes
to the dock if it goes well.

Your station:
- Pell: the Loon's helm, the tow on your conscience. You
  know this channel's lie in the dark.
- Marta: the Wren's helm and engine, June beside you,
  Reedy below in blankets. She was dry aground; a boat
  that's been on the sand can change her mind about that.
- Tobin: the Wren's stern — the tow line's watch, the
  quarter, and your own shaking hands to mind.

~100 words, in your voice. Post your part of the run.
